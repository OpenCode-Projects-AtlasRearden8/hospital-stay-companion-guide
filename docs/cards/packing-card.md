# The Packing Card

> **One page, printable, hospital-stay packing list.** For planned
> stays, run through it the night before the night before. For
> surprise stays, hand it (or text it) to the scout heading to your
> house. Full explanations live in
> [chapter 1](../01-packing.md); this card is the no-prose version.

## Bring

- [ ] Photo ID + insurance card (+ pharmacy cards)
- [ ] **Medications LIST** — every drug, dose, time + vitamins and
  supplements (ask whether to bring the bottles; different hospitals
  want different things)
- [ ] Allergies and past reactions, written down
- [ ] Glasses + case, hearing aids + batteries, denture cup (labeled)
- [ ] Phone + charger + long cable; contact numbers on paper
- [ ] Toothbrush, toothpaste, deodorant, comb, lip balm, lotion
- [ ] One comfort of home (photo, small book)
- [ ] Loose front-fastening clothes to go home in
- [ ] Small notebook + pen (you'll take notes — see the
      [teach-back card](teach-back-card.md))
- [ ] Earplugs / eye mask
- [ ] A few dollars for vending machines (small amounts only)

## Scout (surprise-stay) extras

- [ ] Water bottle + spare layer for *the scout*
- [ ] Anything time-stamped: hospital name, floor/room, arrival time
- [ ] The patient's glasses/hearing aids — labeled, in their cases

## Leave home (or send home with family)

- [ ] Valuables and most jewelry (wedding band is the usual keep)
- [ ] Cash beyond a few dollars; all cards but one
- [ ] The pill cabinet itself — the *list* covers it
- [ ] Alcohol or any outside food/drink without asking the care team
- [ ] Anything you would truly grieve losing

---

**Bag size rule:** small enough to fit under a chair. **List rule:**
the Meds List is the document everyone in the family can find in ten
seconds (a phone photo of it counts as a second copy).

*Education only, never medical advice — the care team's own prep
instructions for a planned stay always override this card.*
